## Executive Summary

The **SmartSolar Water Heater** is an innovative renewable energy solution designed for Solar-R-Us (SRU) LLC. The product combines advanced solar thermal heating technology with a mobile application that enables customers to monitor, control, and optimize their water heating systems remotely.

This project simulates the complete product management lifecycle—from market opportunity assessment and product vision creation to user story development, beta testing, launch planning, and demand generation strategy. The objective is to address rising energy costs, improve energy efficiency, reduce carbon emissions, and create a differentiated customer experience through smart technology integration. 

---

# Problem Statement

Consumers and businesses face increasing utility costs, environmental regulations, and growing pressure to adopt sustainable energy solutions. Traditional water heating systems consume significant energy and provide limited visibility into performance and efficiency.

Although solar water heating solutions exist, many customers encounter barriers such as:

* High installation costs
* Long installation waiting periods
* Complex maintenance requirements
* Limited monitoring capabilities
* Poor user experience

SRU identified an opportunity to launch a next-generation SmartSolar Water Heater that combines high-efficiency solar heating with mobile application control, delivering measurable energy savings and a seamless customer experience. 

---

# Product Vision

> To empower homeowners and businesses with an intelligent, energy-efficient water heating solution that reduces utility expenses, minimizes environmental impact, and provides complete system visibility through a user-friendly mobile application.

### Elevator Pitch

For environmentally conscious homeowners and small-to-medium businesses facing high utility bills, the SmartSolar Water Heater provides an advanced solar thermal energy solution capable of reducing heating costs by **50–80%** while delivering a **3–6 year payback period**. Unlike traditional systems requiring expensive installations and lengthy deployment timelines, SmartSolar offers affordability, simplicity, and smart mobile control.

# User Stories

### User Story 1 – Residential Customer

**As a residential customer,** I want to manage system temperature and enable low-energy consumption settings so that I can maximize savings on my monthly utility bill.

#### Acceptance Criteria

* Current water temperature is displayed accurately.
* Users can adjust target temperatures.
* Energy-saving mode can be enabled with a single action. 

---

### User Story 2 – Commercial Customer

**As a commercial buyer,** I want to monitor water flow and storage capacity so that I can ensure uninterrupted hot water availability and maximize operational efficiency.

#### Acceptance Criteria

* Real-time water flow monitoring.
* Accurate storage level visibility.
* Intuitive dashboard experience. 

---

### User Story 3 – New User Onboarding

**As a new user,** I want a simple setup experience so that I can connect my system without requiring technical assistance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

* Easy application installation.
* Successful hardware pairing.
* Guided onboarding process.
